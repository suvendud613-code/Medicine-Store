Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions

How do I place an order?+

Simply browse our products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can pay online or choose cash on delivery.

What is the delivery time?+

We deliver within 24 hours for in-city orders and 2-3 business days for other locations. Express delivery is also available.

Do I need a prescription?+

Over-the-counter medicines don't require a prescription. For prescription medicines, you can upload your prescription during checkout.

What is the return policy?+

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for unopened products. Contact our support team to initiate a return.

Are the medicines authentic?+

Yes, all our medicines are 100% authentic, sourced directly from authorized distributors and manufacturers. We are a licensed pharmacy.
